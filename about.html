About me

My name is Dylan Tran and I am currently pursuing my Masters in Cybersecurity and I want to work as a Cybersecurity Analyst.

Background

I have recently graduated from Virginia Commonwealth University with my Bachelors in Computer Science and I am now pursuing my Masters in Cybersecurity at Old Dominion University.

Interests

I would like to explore the position of Cybersecurity Analyst. It is like a real life game where you use monitering tools to find suspicious activity and how to prevent ans stop it from happening.

Career goals

In the next two to five years, I see myself working as a Cybersecurity in a security operations center, investigating threats and responding to incidents, while I finish my M.S. in Cybersecurity at Old Dominion University. Along the way, I plan to earn my Security+ and CySA+ certifications and build hands-on experience through internships and lab work. Long term, I want to grow into a senior analyst or incident response role where I can turn technical findings into risk decisions for an organization.
