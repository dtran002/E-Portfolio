Building security in layers

Welcome! I'm Dylan Tran, an M.S. Cybersecurity student at Old Dominion University working toward a career as a security analyst. This semester I looked at networks from both sides: I configured pfSense firewall rules to block ICMP and FTP traffic, ran Nmap reconnaissance against a protected server, and attempted an EternalBlue exploit with Metasploit, only to watch the firewall stop it cold. This portfolio shows what those labs taught me about defense in depth, why reading tool output carefully matters as much as running the tool, and how I think about the threats coming next."
